# Spray Foam SEO — Netlify-ready website

## Deploy
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. In Netlify, choose Add new project → Deploy manually and upload the extracted folder (the folder containing index.html).
3. Enable form detection, deploy and submit a test form. Configure email notifications for submissions in Netlify.
4. Connect your purchased domain, then replace `https://example.com` in canonical tags and `sitemap.xml` with your actual domain.
5. Replace placeholder contact information with real contact details, review privacy/terms, and verify claims before launch.

## Pages
Homepage, 7 individual service pages, services hub, free audit, contact, about, approach, resources, 2 articles, privacy, terms and thank-you.

## Imagery
All site illustrations are bundled in `assets/images` as local lightweight SVG assets. They communicate insulation plus marketing dashboards and are not presented as real client photos or case studies. Replace or supplement with properly licensed real spray-foam project photos when available. No external image dependency.

## Forms
Netlify HTML forms with honeypot and consent checkbox. Forms only work after a successful Netlify deployment; configure submission notifications and test.

## SEO
Houston is a target market, not a claimed physical office. Sitemap and canonical URLs contain `example.com` until the domain is known. Google Search Console and analytics must be connected after deployment.
